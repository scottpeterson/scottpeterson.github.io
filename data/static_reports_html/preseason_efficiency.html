Method

The model is a ridge regression with 10 features, fitted on 410 teams. 2024-25 inputs predict 2025-26 adjEm. The same inputs from 2025-26 produce the 2026-27 projection.

Ten-fold cross-validation picks the penalty. It also drops any feature that does not lower the out-of-fold error. The prior margin, the three transfer features, and the coaching change stay in by design.

Out-of-fold root mean square error (RMSE) is 8.15 points of adjEm. The baseline, last season's margin shrunk toward the mean, scores 9.50 (+1.35). In-sample R² is 0.80.

Returning production counts for more on a strong team. Returning every minute, point, and assist instead of half is worth +13.6 points of adjEm for a team that finished at +30, +10.7 for a team at 0, and +7.8 for a team at -30. The lost-production term sets that slope: it multiplies the share of scoring that left by the prior adjEm.

The split of the projected margin between offense and defense is an assumption. 2024-25 has no adjO or adjD, so the change in margin is applied half to each side on top of the 2025-26 values.

Tempo is a one-variable regression: adjT = -2.2 + 1.03 times the prior adjT.

Barthag is adjOk divided by adjOk plus adjDk. The exponent k = 4.7 is the least-squares fit to 2025-26 win percentage over 413 teams. Torvik uses 11.5 for Division I men.

Dropped by cross-validation: region_lost.

Star departures are counted: postseason All-Americans and all-region players from 2025-26 who are not on the 2026-27 roster. On the 2024-25 to 2025-26 pair the fitted effect was positive, because teams that lost a star beat the turnover-only projection on average.

A departing star cannot be a bonus, so that coefficient is capped at zero. The departure is priced through the points, assists, and lost-production terms.
