Column Legend

Region Region is the team's NCAA Division III region, numbered 1 to 10.
Win% Win% is the team's 2025-26 winning percentage.
SOS SOS is the team's 2025-26 strength of schedule.
NPI NPI is the team's 2025-26 NPI.
Height Height is the team's 2025-26 average height, weighted by minutes played.
Returning Returning is the share of last season's points scored by players on this season's roster.
Projected Preseason Rank Projected Preseason Rank is the team's projected rank at the start of the 2026-27 season.
Safe Losses Threshold Safe Losses Threshold is the most losses a team can have and still be safe for NCAA Tournament selection.
Dangerous Dangerous is the number of losses that puts the team at risk of missing the NCAA Tournament.
