How the checks work
  • Conference opponents can't meet in the first round. The page refuses that drop.
  • Amber warnings: a first-round rematch of a nonconference game played through Selection Sunday, seed lines that differ from the slot, teams from the same region, a host outside the top 16 overall seeds, and possible second-round games between conference opponents or teams that already played.
  • Red errors: a full pod without a host, a team 500 miles or more from its host, more than two top-8 seeds in a quadrant, and two top-8 seeds in the same half of a quadrant, where they would meet before the Elite 8.
  • Sectional check: if every favorite wins its pod, the quadrant's top-4 overall seed should have at least as many of the other three sectional teams within 500 miles as any of those teams has.
  • A guaranteed sectional flight is a pod whose four teams are all 500 miles or more from every other team in the quadrant. Either its winner flies to the sectional, or its winner hosts and the other three fly.
  • Field: 2026 NCAA tournament field, seeded 1-64. Distances: driving miles between campuses.
